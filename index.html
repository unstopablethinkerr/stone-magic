MIND READER

Think of a number from 1 to 16. Tap Left or Right a few times. Then watch me name your number.

1

THINK

Silently pick one of the 16 orbs. Never say it aloud.

2

ANSWER

As they shuffle, say if yours is in the left or right column.

3

REVEAL

I read your mind and name your number.

How does it work?

Each shuffle weaves the two columns together, so the column you name decides where your orb lands next. After three shuffles, every orb has followed its own unique trail of Left and Right answers, and the final column answer settles which of the last two orbs is yours. Four Left/Right answers make 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16 possibilities, exactly the number of orbs.
